## Confirmed mechanics from Studio source inspection

- Seven biomes: Forest, Lake, Desert, Jungle, Tundra, Volcano, Cosmic. Egg grabbing has no cash/rebirth unlock check; deeper areas are primarily challenged by movement, hazards, and guardians.
- Normal hatch times: 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 seconds respectively. Base creature incomes: 5, 30, 150, 800, 4,000, 20,000, 100,000 cash/second.
- Start with 2 incubators and 6 earning pen slots; caps are 6 and 20. Stable capacity is 50. Only pen creatures earn; equipping a pen creature as a mount removes it from the pen.
- Rarity odds: Common 55%, Uncommon 28%, Rare 12%, Epic 4%, Legendary 1%. Income multipliers: 1, 2, 4, 10, 25; Mythic is 100 and comes from Secret Eggs.
- Collection discovery pays 60 seconds of the greater of current total pen income or the discovered creature's income. Selling pays 60 seconds of the sold creature's income.
- Fusion: 3 matching species/rarity creatures become 1 of the next rarity, keeping the strongest mutation. Legendary and Mythic cannot be fused.
- Guardians wake after 1.5 seconds. A carrier passing through another biome can trigger that guardian too. A catch drops the egg, flings, and stuns the player. Matching ability lanes provide escape routes.
- Mount speed uses the fastest equipped creature; abilities combine across equipped mounts. Start with 1 slot, gain another at rebirth 3, and another with ExtraMount pass. Certain species fly; rarity and mutation affect speed and income.
- PvP steals unhatched eggs, never owned creatures. Carrier steal hold: 0.7 seconds; incubator raid hold: 2 seconds; take-back window: 10 seconds. First 15 minutes of accumulated playtime shield players from normal theft and prevent their stealing. Victims get a 60-second robbery cooldown.
- Base lock lasts 60 seconds plus 10 per rebirth; can be renewed immediately after expiry. Joining grants a 30-second lock. Mama checks the new-player shield, not the normal base lock.
- Gear costs 30 seconds of current pen income, minimum 50. Net: 50% slowdown for 3 seconds, 20-second cooldown. Trap: 70% slowdown for 2 seconds, maximum 2 placed, 60-second lifetime. Soda: +25% speed for 20 seconds. Trap code currently does not check target shield status.
- Weather begins every 10 minutes and lasts 2 minutes. Thunderstorm: 30% Shocked chance, 2x income; Golden Hour: 15% Golden, 3x; Blood Moon: 10% Cursed, 5x, guardians 15% faster. Mutation roll occurs on normal nest egg grab.
- Secret Egg appears every 5 minutes somewhere in Desert or deeper; guarantees Mythic, hatches in 60 seconds.
- Mama waits 20 minutes between runs and delays while weather is busy. Warning: 30 seconds; fight: up to 120 seconds. HP: max(300, 150 times connected players). Three clickable weak spots, 1 damage/click, cap 8 accepted clicks/player/second.
- Mama snatches an incubator egg every 6 seconds from the nearest eligible unshielded plot. Defeat returns eggs with hatch progress; escape loses them. Contributors with at least 10 damage earn 1 Lucky Egg; top 3 earn 2. Reward biome uses best currently owned creature biome. Lucky hatch: 30 seconds, rarity advances one tier, capped at Legendary. Stomps currently send effects, not damaging dodge attacks.
- Rebirth 1..4 cash requirements: 100,000; 1 million; 10 million; 100 million, with Jungle/Tundra/Volcano/Cosmic creature requirements. Later cash requirements multiply by 10 each rebirth, with no finite ending.
- Rebirth clears cash, saddle, gear, eggs/pending eggs, and non-equipped creatures. Keeps mounts, purchased capacities, Index, and permanent rebirth rewards. Income multiplier = 1 + 0.5 times rebirth count.
- Offline earnings: half pen income, capped at 8 hours. Egg timers continue offline. Seven-day daily streak gives cash days 1..6 and 15 minutes of luck on day 7.
